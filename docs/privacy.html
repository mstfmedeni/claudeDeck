Privacy

ClaudeDeck has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer doesn't receive any of your data.

ClaudeDeck for iPhone and iPad

  • The app talks only to your own Mac running ClaudeDeck, over your local network or your Tailscale network. The connection is end-to-end encrypted with a key made when you pair; nothing passes through a server of ours.
  • Mac Screen connects straight to your Mac's screen sharing.
  • For notifications, the app gives its push token to your Mac, which sends the notifications through Apple's push service with an APNs key you add yourself.
  • Files, photos and text you send from the phone go only to your Mac.
  • The app collects no data and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. If you install it through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

ClaudeDeck for Mac

  • Your projects, sessions and settings stay on your Mac.
  • The app checks GitHub for updates (Sparkle). It reads GitHub only through the GitHub CLI you sign in to, when you use those features.
  • Agents you add yourself receive what you choose to send them, at the addresses you enter.
